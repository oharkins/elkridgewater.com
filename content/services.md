## Water truck

Bulk delivery of **non-potable** work water — not drinking water. We put water where you need it when a hydrant will not reach: tanks, pits, roads, jobsites, and fire staging.

Common hauls include fire support, farm and ranch water, construction sites, pool filling, animal troughs, dust suppression, and landscaping.

![Elk Ridge water truck on a gravel road](/images/equipment/water-truck.jpg)

## Fire support & suppression

Do not wait until the fire is on the horizon. If you live or work in remote country, a supply of water is one of the things firefighters need most.

- **Rural fire prep** — We fill dry hydrants. If you do not have one, an above-ground pool still gives engines something to draw from. Call while you can still plan the fill.
- **Fire support on the job** — Bulk non-potable water for fire support and suppression work when crews need water on the ground.
- **Agency & government work** — Licensed, insured, Viper-connected, and registered for government contracts when public jobs need a water truck.

## Daylighting trailer

Rent our daylighting trailer alone, or include an operator for vacuum excavation when utilities need to be exposed safely.

![Ring-O-Matic daylighting vacuum excavation trailer](/images/equipment/daylighting-trailer.jpg)

## What these trucks will not haul

These loads are **non-potable** work water only. Household cisterns, drinking tanks, and anything that must be potable are outside what we haul on the water truck.
